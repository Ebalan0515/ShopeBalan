Delivery methods explained

Game Pass delivery

The seller creates a Game Pass and sells it to your account. Usually takes 30–60 minutes, though the payout may stay pending on Roblox's side for about 6–7 days.

Official store delivery

The seller logs into your account (with your permission) and buys Robux directly from the official store. Faster, but the amount per transaction is limited.
