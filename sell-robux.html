How selling works

  1. Set how much Robux you're offering and your asking price
  2. Your listing shows up for buyers browsing Buy Robux
  3. When someone buys, we'll notify you to complete delivery
  4. Get paid once the delivery is confirmed

Delivery methods

Pick whichever method fits how you'll deliver the Robux to a buyer.

Game Pass delivery

You create a Game Pass and sell it to the buyer's account. Usually takes 30–60 minutes, though the payout may stay pending for about 6–7 days on Roblox's side.

Official store delivery

You log into the buyer's account (with their permission) and purchase Robux directly. Faster, but the amount per transaction is limited by the store.

Requirements

  • An active Roblox account
  • Address and contact number saved to your profile
